# @thesealedenvelope: 2-week posting kit (phone sheet)

Built Oct 15, 2026. Nothing is posted or scheduled. 30 videos (9:16, 1080x1920, with audio, 16-28 s). 17 fictional stories, 10 interview re-edits, 3 app demos. Each has a matching `_cover.jpg`.

## Before Day 1
- **Warm-up (2-3 days):** create the account (separate email/phone from the main one). For 2-3 days, don't post. Just watch, like, and comment in the intuition / remote viewing / night-stories niche. Set the bio to: "Short stories about intuition. Some fiction, some history. Try a session ↓" plus the `ct=tt_alt` App Store link.
- Dates below assume you create the account Fri Oct 16, warm it up Fri-Sun, and post Day 1 on **Mon Oct 19**. If you start later, keep the Day numbers and shift the dates.
- **Before the re-edits go live:** decide on the American Alchemy permission email. It's still unsent, and this account doubles the exposure (see the plan, §0). The JRE and Commonwealth Club clips have the same rights question.
- **Day 7 and Day 14 mornings** are review days in the plan. A story is slotted there anyway; do the review before posting it. After Day 7, swap in a spare (29 or 30) for whichever format is losing.
- Daylight saving time ends Sun Nov 1 (Day 14). The posting times stay 9:12 AM / 7:24 PM on your phone's clock.
- When you upload, pick the cover from the video timeline or upload the `_cover.jpg`. Turn on TikTok's "AI-generated content" label for every fictional story, since their visuals are generated.
